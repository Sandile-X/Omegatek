OmegaHertzEQ

8-Band Fully Parametric & Dynamic Equalizer

A professional-grade parametric equalizer built with JUCE 8. Featuring 8 fully configurable bands with per-band dynamic EQ controls, real-time spectrum analysis, A/B comparison, and a stunning RGB-animated UI. Shape your sound with surgical precision.

technical specs

Band Types

Low Shelf, Bell, High Shelf, Low Pass, High Pass, Notch, All Pass, and Band Pass — all fully selectable per band.

Dynamic Processing

Per-band dynamic EQ with dedicated attack, release, threshold, and ratio controls for surgical frequency-dependent compression.

Spectrum Analyser

Real-time FFT spectrum display with pre, post, and delta viewing modes. See exactly how your EQ shapes the signal.

Band Solo & Mute

Isolate individual bands to fine-tune adjustments, or mute bands to quickly compare with and without processing.

Band Inspector

Dedicated inspector panel for precise numeric editing of every parameter — frequency, gain, Q, and dynamic controls.

Loudness Metering

Integrated LUFS, peak, and RMS metering bridge for professional loudness monitoring during mastering sessions.
