OmegaHertzEQ

Eight bands. One focused EQ workflow.

OmegaHertzEQ combines precision parametric EQ, per-band dynamic processing, and a clear visual workspace for fast, intentional tone shaping. Shape a mix, tame a resonance, or build a wider mastering move without leaving the curve.

8 Configurable Bands Dynamic EQ on Every Band Pre, Post & Sidechain Views Stereo / Mid / Side / L / R A/B Compare + Undo/Redo Peak, RMS & Loudness
Download for Windows Explore the Features

Windows x64 · VST3 + Standalone · 8-band parametric EQ

Precision When You Need It

Shape the whole picture — or the exact problem

Start on the interactive spectrum canvas, where every band is visible at once. Drag a numbered node to move frequency and gain together, use the mouse wheel to refine Q, and switch between focused ±18 dB and extended ±24 dB gain ranges. Choose Low Cut, Low Shelf, Peak, High Shelf, High Cut, or Notch filtering, with 12, 24, or 48 dB/octave cut slopes where they matter.

Every layer of the curve — EQ, spectrum, dynamics, and metering — visible at once.

Dynamics Where the Music Needs It

Make a band react, not just sit still

Each EQ band includes its own Dynamic EQ controls: threshold, attack, release, ratio, and dynamic boost. Target harshness only when it appears, hold a vocal in place, or make tonal moves respond to the programme material. Gain-reduction feedback on the canvas keeps the action visible as you work.

Threshold, attack, release, ratio — dialled in per band, live.

Make Decisions With Context

Hear it. See it. Compare it.

Switch the real-time spectrum display between pre-EQ, post-EQ, and sidechain views. Use A/B snapshots for instant comparisons, and use undo / redo freely while you explore. Integrated peak, RMS, and loudness metering, plus Auto Gain and master bypass, make level-aware decisions part of the same workflow.

Pre / Post / Sidechain A/B Snapshots Free Undo / Redo Peak / RMS / LUFS Auto Gain Master Bypass

Built for Real Sessions

Start fast, then make it yours

OmegaHertzEQ opens with a practical 8-band layout and includes 49 factory presets for common starting points. Work in Stereo, Mid, Side, Left, or Right per band; solo or mute bands while hunting down a frequency; then resize the interface to suit the session.

49 Factory Presets

A full bank of starting points across Factory, Mastering, Vocal, Headphone Correction, Dynamic EQ, and Mzansi Mix categories.

Per-Band Solo & Mute

Isolate individual bands to fine-tune adjustments, or mute bands to quickly compare with and without processing.

Stereo / Mid / Side / L / R

Route each band independently — keep sub-bass mono, put air on the Side, and leave the rest of the field untouched.

Resizable Interface

Scale the UI from 780×520 up to 2560×1440 — work small in a crowded session, or blow it up on a second monitor.

Windows x64

VST3 for DAW use, plus a Standalone application for working directly against an audio device.

Mzansi Mix Presets

12 factory presets built from the ground up for South African music. From Amapiano log drums and Gqom drum buses to gospel choirs, maskandi guitar, kwaito low end, lekompo synth buses, and a club mastering chain — every preset is a research-backed starting context, not a fixed prescription. Load it, listen, and dial it your way.
