## Section: Precision when you need it

### Shape the whole picture — or the exact problem

Start on the interactive spectrum canvas, where every band is visible at once. Drag a numbered node to move frequency and gain together, use the mouse wheel to refine Q, and switch between focused ±18 dB and extended ±24 dB gain ranges. Choose Low Cut, Low Shelf, Peak, High Shelf, High Cut, or Notch filtering, with 12, 24, or 48 dB/octave cut slopes where they matter.

**Image:** `omegahertzeq-hero-v2.png`

**Suggested caption:** Eight colour-coded bands give you a complete view of the curve from 20 Hz to 20 kHz.

## Section: Dynamics where the music needs it

### Make a band react, not just sit still

Each EQ band includes its own Dynamic EQ controls: threshold, attack, release, ratio, and dynamic boost. Target harshness only when it appears, hold a vocal in place, or make tonal moves respond to the programme material. Gain-reduction feedback on the canvas keeps the action visible as you work.

**Image:** `omegahertzeq-dynamic-eq-feature-v2.png`

**Suggested caption:** Band 2 selected with the full Dynamic EQ inspector in view.

---

## Section: Make decisions with context

### Hear it. See it. Compare it.

Switch the real-time spectrum display between pre-EQ, post-EQ, and sidechain views. Use A/B snapshots for instant comparisons, and use undo / redo freely while you explore. Integrated peak, RMS, and loudness metering, plus Auto Gain and master bypass, make level-aware decisions part of the same workflow.

## Section: Built for real sessions

### Start fast, then make it yours

OmegaHertzEQ opens with a practical 8-band layout and includes 49 factory presets for common starting points. Work in Stereo, Mid, Side, Left, or Right per band; solo or mute bands while hunting down a frequency; then resize the interface to suit the session.

**Feature list:**

- Resizable interface from 780 × 520 to 2560 × 1440
